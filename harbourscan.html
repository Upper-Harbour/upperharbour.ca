Frequently asked questions

Is my data stored when I run a HarbourScan?

No. The initial scan runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers or stored anywhere. You can optionally email yourself a summary of your results, but the scan itself is completely private.

How long does a HarbourScan take?

Most organizations complete the scan in under 10 minutes. You select your tools from a database of 753 mapped SaaS products, and HarbourScan instantly maps each one to its parent jurisdiction and CLOUD Act status.

What do I need to run a scan?

Just a list of the SaaS tools your organization uses. No login, no account, no access to your systems. You select tools from our database and HarbourScan does the rest.

What format are the paid deliverables?

The Sovereignty Snapshot is delivered as a PDF report within 5 business days. The Compliance Documentation package includes all Snapshot deliverables plus TIA guidance, a Register of Processing Activities, remediation roadmap, and board-ready compliance documents.

Do I need a Sovereignty Snapshot if I already ran the free scan?

The free scan shows your exposure. The Snapshot documents it. If a board member, regulator, procurement officer, or partner asks about your data sovereignty posture, the Snapshot is the PDF you hand them.

What's the difference between the CLOUD Act page and this tool?

The CLOUD Act page explains the law. HarbourScan tells you how it applies to your specific stack. One is education, the other is assessment.
