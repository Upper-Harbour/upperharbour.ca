Micrologic Canadian Data Sovereignty Analysis

Micrologic is the only Canadian sovereign cloud recognized by Gartner in the Operational Sovereignty category. Founded in Quebec City in 1983, the company has been operating sovereign cloud infrastructure for over a decade — well before digital sovereignty became a mainstream topic. 100% Canadian-owned, with data subject exclusively to Canadian law.

Operational sovereignty

Micrologic provides total sovereignty across four dimensions: data, operations, legal framework, and technical access. The cloud infrastructure is entirely hosted, operated, and controlled in Canada, without access or management by foreign entities. The Cirrus cloud platform is powered by VMware and supports IaaS, storage, disaster recovery, backup, cybersecurity, and AI workloads.

Certifications and recognition

ISO 27001:2013, ISO 27017:2015, and ISO 27018:2019 certified. Qualified for Public Services and Procurement Canada's SaaS catalogue. Recognized by Gartner for Operational Sovereignty — the only Canadian provider to receive this distinction. CEO Stéphane Garneau named CEO of the Year by Les Affaires and the Quebec Technology Association.

Growth trajectory

Micrologic has announced a $150 million pan-Canadian expansion plan funded by private and public investment. Over the past decade, the company has grown revenues more than tenfold and expanded from a small team to hundreds of experts. The expansion signals that Canadian sovereign cloud is not a niche offering — it's scaling to meet enterprise and government demand.

Why it matters for compliance

For Quebec organizations subject to Law 25, Micrologic eliminates the need for a Transfer Impact Assessment at the infrastructure layer — because there is no cross-border transfer. For federal organizations, the PSPC catalogue qualification means Micrologic is procurement-ready. For any Canadian organization, it provides an infrastructure foundation with zero foreign jurisdictional exposure.
