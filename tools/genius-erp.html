Is Genius ERP CLOUD Act exposed for Canadian organizations?

Not directly. Cogismaq International Inc. (doing business as Genius Solutions) is incorporated in Quebec City, Quebec, Canada. The company is wholly Canadian-owned — four partners, all involved in day-to-day operations. There is no foreign parent company, no foreign investors with board seats, and no foreign controlling shareholders. As a Canadian company, Cogismaq is not directly subject to the US CLOUD Act.

Cogismaq does operate a US subsidiary — Genius Solutions Enterprises Inc. — which is US-registered with US employees. This subsidiary supports American manufacturing customers. The existence of a US subsidiary does not make the Canadian parent CLOUD Act exposed — the CLOUD Act targets companies incorporated in or subject to US jurisdiction for their own data, but the Canadian parent's Canadian-hosted customer data is not automatically in scope through a subsidiary relationship.

However, Genius ERP is hosted on Microsoft Azure — a US-owned infrastructure provider. This creates the same indirect exposure pathway seen with other Canadian companies like Hootsuite and FreshBooks: data on Azure Canada infrastructure is operated by Microsoft (US), which could be compelled to provide access. The Canadian data residency mitigates but does not eliminate this pathway.

Corporate structure

Cogismaq International Inc. (also known as Groupe Conseil Cogismaq) was founded in 1989 in Quebec City — over 35 years in business. The company registered the GENIUS ERP trademark with the Canadian Intellectual Property Office in 2017. Government of Canada funding ($242,000 through Canada Economic Development) supported international commercialization. The City of Québec partnered with Genius Solutions through Vision Entrepreneuriale for US market expansion.

Cogismaq is privately held, wholly Canadian-owned. The four partners are all involved in operations — no passive foreign ownership, no venture capital with board control, no publicly traded stock that could change ownership. This is the strongest ownership structure possible from a sovereignty perspective.

Manufacturing data sensitivity

ERP systems for manufacturers contain highly sensitive operational data: production schedules, bill of materials, costing and pricing data, customer orders, supplier relationships, inventory levels, engineering specifications, quality records, and financial data (accounting, payroll, invoicing). For manufacturers in defence, aerospace, or critical infrastructure, this data may have national security implications. Using a Canadian-jurisdiction ERP vendor with Canadian data hosting significantly reduces the sovereignty exposure compared to US-parented alternatives like NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle.

Quebec Law 25

Quebec manufacturers using Genius ERP benefit from the Canadian jurisdiction for Law 25 compliance. The TIA is simplified when both the vendor and the data hosting are Canadian. The Azure infrastructure creates a secondary consideration (Microsoft is US-parented), but the primary data controller (Cogismaq) is Canadian — a materially stronger position than using a US-incorporated ERP vendor.

Alberta POPA

Alberta public bodies or manufacturers using Genius ERP benefit from the Canadian jurisdiction. The PIA is simpler when the vendor is Canadian-incorporated. The PIA Research Tool generates these answers automatically.

The US subsidiary question

Genius Solutions Enterprises Inc. is US-registered with US employees, supporting American manufacturing customers. For Canadian customers using Genius ERP: your data is managed by the Canadian parent (Cogismaq International Inc.) on Canadian-configured Azure infrastructure. The US subsidiary serves the US market — it does not change the jurisdiction of the Canadian parent's operations or the Canadian customers' data handling.

Technical Architecture

▾

Cloud hosting

Genius ERP is hosted on Microsoft Azure in Canada East/Central and US East/Central regions. Canadian customers can be configured for Canadian-only data residency on Azure Canada. The product is available on the Azure Marketplace. Cogismaq worked with CRIM (Centre de recherche informatique de Montréal) on their cloud migration from monolithic to modular architecture.

Product scope

Genius ERP is a complete manufacturing ERP covering: CRM and sales quoting, product engineering (with CAD integration), production planning and scheduling, shop floor management (MES), materials management and inventory (MRP), job costing, purchasing, accounting and finance, quality management, and field service. Designed specifically for make-to-order, engineer-to-order, custom-to-order, and assemble-to-order manufacturers.

Market position

Genius Solutions serves hundreds of manufacturers across North America. Founded 1989 (35+ years). Quebec City headquarters. Government of Canada funded ($242K for international commercialization). City of Québec partnership through Vision Entrepreneuriale. Available on Microsoft Azure Marketplace and Microsoft AppSource.

Frequently Asked Questions

▾

Is Genius ERP subject to the US CLOUD Act?

Not directly. Cogismaq International Inc. is incorporated in Quebec City, Canada. Wholly Canadian-owned (4 partners, all in operations). The company has a US subsidiary (Genius Solutions Enterprises Inc.) for the US market, but Canadian customers' data is managed by the Canadian parent.

Does Genius ERP offer Canadian data residency?

Yes. Hosted on Azure Canada East/Central. Canadian customers can have data stored exclusively in Canada. The Azure infrastructure is Microsoft-operated, which creates an indirect exposure pathway — but the primary data controller is Canadian.

Who owns Cogismaq / Genius Solutions?

Cogismaq International Inc. is privately held, wholly Canadian-owned by four partners who are all involved in day-to-day operations. No foreign parent, no foreign investors with board seats, no public stock exchange listing. Founded 1989 in Quebec City.

What types of manufacturers use Genius ERP?

Custom manufacturers: make-to-order, engineer-to-order, custom-to-order, and assemble-to-order. Sectors include metal fabrication, wood, fiberglass, heavy machinery, printing, and more. Hundreds of manufacturers across North America.

How does Genius ERP compare to US ERPs for sovereignty?

Genius ERP is the only major manufacturing ERP that is Canadian-incorporated with Canadian data hosting. US alternatives (NetSuite, Epicor) are fully CLOUD Act exposed. SAP (German) is better jurisdictionally than US options but is still foreign-owned. For Canadian manufacturers, Genius ERP provides the strongest sovereignty position.
