Sovereignty Migration

Rebuild your SaaS stack for Canadian sovereignty.

Most Canadian organizations run on software controlled by foreign companies, subject to foreign laws, and exposed to foreign data access regimes. Moving off that stack requires more than swapping one tool for another — it requires a structured overhaul of your entire SaaS posture.

Upper Harbour provides the jurisdictional assessment. Our implementation partners handle the migration. You get a sovereign stack with documentation that satisfies your regulator, your board, and your procurement office.

Why organizations are migrating now

Regulatory pressure, board scrutiny, and procurement reform are converging. Organizations that understand their exposure now have time to migrate on their own terms.

Quebec
Law 25 — Transfer Impact Assessments

Quebec’s privacy law requires a Transfer Impact Assessment before personal information leaves the province. Every US-parented SaaS tool in your stack triggers this obligation.

Alberta
POPA — Mandatory PIAs

Alberta’s new public-sector privacy rules require Privacy Impact Assessments that explicitly analyze CLOUD Act and foreign jurisdiction exposure for every tool handling personal information.

Federal
Procurement & Board Mandates

Federal procurement officers and boards of directors are asking the same question: which foreign government can compel disclosure of our data? If you don’t have an answer documented, you have a problem.

How the migration works

Four phases, each with a clear deliverable. You know exactly where you are at every stage.

01
Jurisdictional Audit

We map every SaaS tool in your stack to its parent company, jurisdiction of incorporation, and CLOUD Act exposure status using the Sovereignty Index.

02
Risk Prioritization

Not every exposed tool is equally urgent. We rank your exposure by data sensitivity, regulatory trigger, and operational dependency to build a sequenced migration plan.

03
Alternative Identification

For each tool being migrated, we identify sovereign alternatives that meet your functional requirements — Canadian-jurisdiction commercial tools from the 142 in our Index, Canadian-hosted open-source alternatives you can self-manage, or custom-built solutions where no off-the-shelf option fits.

04
Implementation

Our implementation partners handle the technical migration — data transfer, user provisioning, integration reconfiguration — while we provide ongoing jurisdictional oversight.
