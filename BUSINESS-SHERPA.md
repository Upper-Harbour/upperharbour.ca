## What Upper Harbour Is

Upper Harbour is a Canadian technology sovereignty intelligence firm. It maps how Canadian organizations depend on foreign-controlled technology infrastructure — who owns it, which laws govern it, and what that means for compliance, procurement, and national security.

The core insight: **data residency ≠ data sovereignty.** A tool can store data in Canada while the parent company remains subject to US legal process. The physical location of the server is irrelevant if the company that operates it can be compelled by a foreign government to hand over data.

Upper Harbour's research is public and free. The revenue model is sovereignty intelligence services — assessments, compliance documentation, and ongoing monitoring — sold via scoping calls after organizations use the free tools.

**Positioning (updated February 2026):** Upper Harbour is not a compliance tool. It is a sovereignty intelligence platform. The research and data are the moat. Compliance documentation is the revenue model. The distinction matters: compliance tools compete on features. Sovereignty intelligence competes on depth and authority.
