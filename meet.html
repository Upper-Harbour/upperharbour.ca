About Upper Harbour

The AI privacy agent for Canadian regulated organizations.

Upper Harbour helps privacy officers, compliance teams, and small-to-mid-sized Canadian organizations produce defensible PIAs, TIAs, breach assessments, and Quebec Law 25 documentation in a fraction of the time — with audit-ready output and a verified Canadian sovereignty data layer.

The platform is built on the Sovereignty Index — 768 SaaS tools cross-referenced against parent jurisdictions, CLOUD Act exposure, and Canadian data residency — and grounded in the verbatim text of every major Canadian privacy statute.
