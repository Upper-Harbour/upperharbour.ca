How to read these

Ember on Charcoal (current)

Distinctive in Canadian law. Reads as modern professional services, not white-shoe firm. The orange does the heavy lifting — if you don't trust the orange, this whole approach falls apart.

Oxblood on Cream

What 80% of Canadian firms already look like. Safe, traditional, immediately legible as "law firm." But Ember now looks like a smaller version of Stikeman or Blakes — the positioning differentiation evaporates.

Forest & Brass

European private bank energy. Heritage without nationalism. Reads as senior, expensive, deliberate. Risk: starts to feel like a wealth management firm or a whisky brand more than a law firm.

Mono with Ember Accent

The hedge. Charcoal does the seriousness work, ember accent does the differentiation work. Closest to Stripe / Linear / modern fintech aesthetic. Probably the safest "modern professional" move that still keeps Ember's name coherent with its color.
