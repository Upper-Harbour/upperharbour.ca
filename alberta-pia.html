Alberta POPA · PIA Research Tool

The OIPC requires the template. We provide the answers.

Alberta public bodies must now submit Privacy Impact Assessments to the OIPC using a mandatory template. The template asks jurisdictional questions about every SaaS tool you use — parent company, CLOUD Act status, data residency, encryption. Most organizations can't answer them.

Select your tools from our 753-tool database. We generate the pre-written answers for Sections F, G, and H2 — ready to copy directly into your OIPC submission.
