Common questions

What data does the API return?

Each classification includes canonical tool name, aliases, parent company (legal name), ultimate-parent jurisdiction, CLOUD Act exposure flag, Canadian data residency availability, risk score (0–10 scale), and links to primary sources. The Canadian alternatives endpoint returns a ranked list of sovereign alternatives with the same structure.

How current is the classification data?

The corpus is maintained continuously. SEC EDGAR integration catches US public-company M&A in near-real-time. Canadian private-company rebrands and ownership changes are swept quarterly. All tiers get daily data refreshes; Enterprise subscribers can additionally request specific classifications on a 10-business-day turnaround.

Can we white-label or resell the classification data?

Not at the standard tiers. Classifications are licensed for use as part of your product, not for resale as a standalone data product. Custom arrangements can be discussed for enterprise integrations — we'll scope it during the technical call.

What happens if we exceed our tier cap?

Starter has no cap — you're billed $0.003 per call. Pro includes 10,000 calls; overage billed at $0.003/call above that. Enterprise has no cap. No surprise invoices — you set alerts at any usage threshold.

We're just starting out and don't know our volume. Where should we land?

Start on Starter — pay-as-you-go at $0.003/call, $25/month minimum. Gives you time to prove the integration and understand your call volume before committing to a monthly tier. Most partners who exceed ~8,000 calls/month find Pro becomes cheaper; we'll surface that in your quarterly review and you can upgrade any time.

How does exit work?

60 days notice from either party. On termination, API keys are rotated, cached data in your systems must be flushed within 30 days. Any pre-paid annual subscription is pro-rated. No penalties for exiting.
