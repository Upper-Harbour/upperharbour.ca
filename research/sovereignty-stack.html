The Sovereignty Stack

Canada is securing the bottom of the technology stack. The top — where organizations encounter foreign jurisdiction daily — remains unaddressed.

Scroll to explore↓
The full stack

Digital systems run on four layers.

From physical servers to SaaS applications. Each layer has its own ownership, jurisdiction, and legal exposure.

Where policy focuses

Sovereignty policy targets the bottom two.

Sovereign cloud RFI. $2B AI compute. Bell-Hypertec. The March 2026 RFI requires providers and their ultimate parent corporations are not subject to foreign compelled-disclosure laws.

Where exposure sits

The top two layers have no equivalent policy.

82% of SaaS tools used by Canadian organizations are under foreign jurisdiction. 12 entire categories have zero Canadian-owned vendors.

The jurisdiction question

Infrastructure can be domestic. Software remains governed abroad.

Sovereign infrastructure does not automatically produce sovereign systems.
