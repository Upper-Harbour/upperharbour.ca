When you need more than a report

Our self-serve assessments document your exposure. They’re the right starting point for most organizations. But some situations need hands-on expertise: your stack is complex, your regulatory environment is layered, your board wants a defensible position, or you’re ready to actually migrate sensitive workloads to sovereign alternatives.

That’s what consulting is for. Every engagement is led by Joshua van Es — the same person who built the Sovereignty Index, researched every tool page, and wrote the compliance guides.

Frequently asked questions

Who leads the engagement?

Joshua van Es leads every consulting engagement personally. He built the Sovereignty Index (755+ tools), wrote every tool analysis page, and has a corporate law background. You’re working directly with the person who built the platform.

What industries do you work with?

Any Canadian organization that uses SaaS tools and has compliance obligations under PIPEDA, Law 25, FIPPA, PIPA, or POPA. Most common clients are in professional services, healthcare, finance, education, and government.

How is this different from a privacy consultant?

Privacy consultants do broad compliance work. We specialize exclusively in technology sovereignty — which tools are exposed, what the jurisdictional risks are, and what alternatives exist. We have the deepest dataset on SaaS vendor jurisdictions in Canada.

Can I start with a $99 report and upgrade later?

Yes. Many consulting clients start with a self-serve assessment to understand the landscape, then engage consulting when they need hands-on help with remediation and migration. The $99 report isn’t wasted — it gives us a baseline to work from.
